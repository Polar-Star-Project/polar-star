Number from ICE's page for this facility, last read Nov 16, 2026.

Help here is free. The ICE locator, the court line and the hotlines below cost nothing. Never pay anyone who calls or messages offering to find the person, speed up the case or arrange a bond. Only a lawyer or a representative accredited by the Justice Department can give legal advice; a “notario” cannot.

What to do now

  1. Search ICE's locator
    You need the person's A-number and country of birth, or their full name, country and date of birth. The locator can take days to update after a move, so check again. Open the ICE locator
  2. Free hotline for people held
    Freedom for Immigrants runs a free national hotline that people in detention can call from a facility phone. Family and friends can write to hotline@freedomforimmigrants.org. Hotline details
  3. Find free legal help
    The Justice Department lists free legal service providers by state. Open the list
